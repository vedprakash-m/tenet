| T1 | Major | TN-3, §5.1 link rule, E4, E5, §6 | The rule: an `asset`, `data` or `abuse` entry is linked when it is a `constraint`, or an `example` or `invariant` "with a test file". ## Verdict: needs revision

Required before sign-off: T1 (the link rule contradicts how invariants and tests are stored), T2 (claim strength for constraints), T3 and T4 (Deep drafting; conflict with `trait-detection.md` §5 item 8 and with red-first), T5 (E12 without behavior), T6 (weakening by correction), T7 and T8 (what `INTENT.md` publishes; migration inventory), T9 (wording). Option A (tagged elements) remains the smaller design, but T1 shows it is not as small as stated: the "invariant with a test" case needs a definition, and T4 may favor option B (a separate record outside the basis hash). Implementation is, as the spec says, blocked on QLT-11 wiring and the migration gate; none of these findings changes that. Q1 to Q6 remain the owner's.
