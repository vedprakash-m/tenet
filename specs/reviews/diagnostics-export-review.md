## Verified (no finding)

- `DECISIONS` has no `export` kind; `InspectInput.view` has no `export` value; `decide` calls `workOf` before any kind runs and throws `handle-required` or `handle-unknown` (`verbs/decide.ts`, `verbs/common.ts:27`).
- A thrown refusal inside the pipeline is rendered without `tx.call`, so a refused `decide export` appends no journal record (`pipeline.ts` `fail`, `pipeline-render.ts:108` is the only `tx.call`). The spec's E6 to E8 premise holds.
- `recover-effects.ts` is 263 lines against the 265-line cap (`tools/coverage.ts:348`); `decisions/cleanup.ts` 1,039 and `decisions/hardening.ts` 2,757 non-whitespace characters match the spec's figures.
- Windows refuses mutating verbs (`pipeline.ts`, `core/risk.ts` `mutationSupported`), so `decide export` cannot run on Windows.
- The journal schema is passthrough for call, intent and result records (`adapters/store-fs.ts:28-33`).
