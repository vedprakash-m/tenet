# CP-0054 requirement origins

On 2026-11-24, the Owner decided that a requirement they wrote has origin `asked` under R-210, with no citation. An archived copy of the requirement is revision history and does not establish its source.

Keep `documented` when a citation identifies a different source that recorded the decision, such as a ledger decision, the original process document, or another specification section. Use `observed` when a requirement merely records existing code or test behavior and cites the relevant lines or test. Record other requirements as `asked` without cites. Historic version links in document headers stay as history.
