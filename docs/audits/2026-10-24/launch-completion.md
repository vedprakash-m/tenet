## 1. Product And Release Boundary

The [PRD](../../../specs/PRD.md) owns meaning, the [technical specification](../../../specs/tech-spec.md) mechanisms, the [UX specification](../../../specs/ux-spec.md) wording, and the [plan](../../../specs/plan.md) sequencing and evidence status.

Tenet adds intent-to-evidence continuity to the user's existing Copilot environment: clarify consequential ambiguity, confirm examples, implement test-first, review independently, preserve human work, report candidate-bound evidence and retain project intent. Its deterministic kernel records binding facts; agents perform engineering and explain them. The host supplies execution, permissions and native delegation. Cooperative instructions do not become preventive controls by being described more strongly.

R1 is a TypeScript local engineering loop for technical builders on an exact qualified VS Code tuple, not the entire vision. Deployment, other ecosystems/qualified hosts, model routing, dashboards and parallel product writers are outside R1. Parallel development of Tenet itself is distinct from its product source-ownership protocol.

Public R1 requires every due Must to pass its mapped suites, every Should to pass or have a signed omission, published residual risks and product/release approval. Never-waivable AC-07/11/16/17/25/27/28 remain in force. No local test count establishes those conditions.
